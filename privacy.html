Privacy policy

Applies to: TradingHubPlatform. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Who uses the app

TradingHubPlatform is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. There are no other users and no servers run by the app.

Google user data the app accesses

The app asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can only create, read, update and delete the folder and the files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in the Google Drive.

How the data is used

  • The app uploads copies of its own settings and work to the owner's Google Drive, and downloads them again only when the owner chooses to bring one back.
  • The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer, in the app's private configuration folder.
  • No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive, and nothing is shared, sold, used for advertising or used to train any model.

How the data is stored and deleted

These files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
