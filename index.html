TradingHubPlatform

A personal research app that keeps a copy of its own settings in its owner's Google Drive.

What it is

TradingHubPlatform is a private stock-market research workstation that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: it has no accounts, no website login and no other users.

What it does with Google Drive

Once a day the app saves a copy of its own settings and work (watchlists, notes, chart drawings and similar settings) into a folder of its own in the owner's Google Drive, and can bring a saved copy back when the owner asks. It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can only see and change the files it created itself, nothing else in the Drive.
